    About

    About CoupeFab

    CoupeFab is a French-first, bilingual resource for plasma cutting and metal fabrication shops in Quebec. The site gathers CAD/CAM guides, a digest of used equipment, and a sheet-price tracker when prices are posted publicly.

    Technical pages are reviewed by Michaël Brassard before they count as published. While the “draft — to be reviewed by Michaël” banner is visible, treat the text as a working draft, not as a cutting instruction.

    We do not sell machines and we do not take a commission on listings for now. There are no affiliate links in place. See the disclosure page.

    Contact

    A question, a correction, or a public listing we should see: write to us. If you would rather have the newsletter, it is here.
