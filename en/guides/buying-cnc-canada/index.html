    Buying a CNC in Canada: new or used, power, certification, import

    Define the need, check voltage and electrical certification, compare new and used, import from the US without surprises, and the checklist for a used plasma table in Quebec.

    Reviewed November 26, 2026 3 min read Level : Beginner DraftWritten by CoupeFab from the official documents cited. Technical review by Michaël Brassard pending.

    Buying a plasma table, router or laser in Canada adds a few questions an American guide does not ask: available voltage (600 V three-phase is common in Canadian industrial buildings), required electrical certification, customs, taxes, freight and in-country service.

    1. Define the need before shopping

    QuestionWhy
    Real materials and thicknesses (80% of the work)Sets the process and power
    Sheet size you buy4 × 8 ft, 5 × 10 ft: the table must take it without recutting
    Volume and precisionAir plasma, high definition, laser?
    Space, access, floorDoors, crane, slab, height
    UtilitiesVoltage, available amperage, dry compressed air, ventilation
    SoftwareCAM and control included? licences transferable?

    2. Power and certification

    • Voltage: confirm your service voltage and phases (120/240 V single-phase, 208 V, 347/600 V three-phase) before choosing the source and table. A transformer adds cost and losses.
    • Certification: in Quebec, electrical equipment must be approved (certified by a recognized body, for example CSA, cUL or cETL) before connection; uncertified equipment may need a special field evaluation. Ask the seller for the nameplate and certificate.
    • Connection: by a master electrician, under the Quebec Construction Code, Chapter V – Electricity.

    Caution

    Many machines sold online from abroad carry no Canadian certification mark. The cost of a special evaluation or rewiring can wipe out the savings.

    3. New or used

    NewUsed
    PriceHigherLower, variable
    Warranty and trainingYesRarely (“as is”)
    TechnologyCurrentControl, source and software sometimes dated
    Lead timePer the makerImmediate, but dismantling and freight at your cost
    RiskLowReduce with a serious inspection

    There are plasma table makers in Quebec and Canada: local service and parts in the country matter as much as price.

    4. Importing from the United States

    1. Check Canadian certification (see above).
    2. Ask whether the product qualifies for CUSMA tariff treatment: the origin of the goods, not the shipping country, determines duty.
    3. Allow for GST and QST on import or purchase (5% and 9.975% in Quebec), brokerage and freight.
    4. Allow for exchange costs and cross-border after-sales service.

    5. Checklist: used plasma table in Quebec

    • Envelope: usable size versus doors, crane and height; real voltage (208 / 460 / 600 V, single or three phase); compressed air and dryer; dust collector or water table in place
    • Source and torch: brand, model, amperage, consumable family, hours if available, working THC; an advertised “severance capacity” is not production quality — ask to see your usual thickness cut
    • Mechanics: backlash in racks or screws, gantry square, rail condition, cables and carriers, rusty slats, leaks on a water table
    • Control and licences: controller (Edge, Vision T5, etc.), backups, CAM software and licences (SheetCam, FastCAM…) included or to rebuy
    • History: retrofits (when, by whom, which parts), parts availability in Canada, original documentation
    • Test cut: watch it cut; measure a coupon (kerf, dross, square); listen for limit switches and arc faults
    • Logistics and contract: as-is terms, dismantling, freight, taxes (GST/QST), availability date; an “ex-works” price is not a delivered price

    See also the dated digest of public listings: Used equipment in Quebec.

    FAQ

    Where to look for used machines in Quebec?

    Dealers and auctioneers (for example Machinerie ALX, Industriel Go / Machinio, Tardif Industrie, Ritchie Bros.) and classifieds. Our used-equipment digest links dated public listings.

    Do I need a permit to install a plasma table?

    The electrical connection is done by a master electrician. Also check your municipality’s and insurer’s requirements (fire, ventilation).
