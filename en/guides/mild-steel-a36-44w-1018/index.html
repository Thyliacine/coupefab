    Mild steel in the shop: A36, 44W, 1018, hot- or cold-rolled, and how to cut it

    The most-cut steel in Quebec: A36 and CSA G40.21 44W/50W, hot-rolled, pickled (HRPO) or cold-rolled sheet, mill scale, plasma/laser/oxyfuel cutting, bending and welding, linked to published prices.

    Reviewed November 26, 2026 3 min read Level : Beginner DraftWritten by CoupeFab from the official documents cited. Technical review by Michaël Brassard pending.

    “Mild steel” means low-carbon steels that are easy to cut, bend and weld. In practice you meet a structural standard (ASTM A36, CSA G40.21 44W or 50W) for plate and shapes, and a flat-product standard for thin sheet (ASTM A1011 hot-rolled, A1008 cold-rolled). Machined bars are often 1018 (see 1018 cold-drawn vs hot-rolled).

    Which designation for what?

    DesignationStandardWhere you see itRemember
    A36ASTM A36Plate, shapes, flat bar (US and Canada)The structural “mild steel” reference
    44W / 50WCSA G40.21Plate and shapes in CanadaThe number = minimum yield in ksi; often dual-certified with A36 / A572
    HR / HRPO (A1011)ASTM A1011Hot-rolled sheet, pickled and oiled (PO)Scale removed in HRPO: better for paint and laser
    CR (A1008)ASTM A1008Cold-rolled sheetSmooth surface, tight tolerances, thin gauges
    Galvanized (A653)ASTM A653Zinc-coated sheet (G60, G90…)Zinc fumes when cutting and welding: ventilation (see Safety and CNESST)

    Cutting mild steel

    • Plasma: the shop king for mild steel; usually air, charts by thickness (see Reading cut charts).
    • Fiber laser: O₂ for thick (oxidized edge), N₂ or air for thin and a clean edge (see Laser: dross and burning).
    • Oxyfuel: very economical on heavy plate (see Oxyfuel cutting).
    • Scale and rust: hurt ohmic sensing and quality; clean the sensing spot if needed.

    Bending and welding

    • Easy bending; minimum radius depends on thickness, grade and rolling direction (see Press brake and Bending: K-factor and deduction).
    • Easy welding with all common processes; clean scale and cutting oxide per the procedure (see Weld preparation).

    Ordering checklist

    • Standard and grade (A36, 44W, A1011 HRPO…)
    • Thickness as gauge and decimal (see Sheet metal gauges)
    • Sheet size (4×8, 5×10, 4×10 ft…)
    • Mill certificate if structural
    • Compare published prices in the price comparison tool

    Common mistakes

    MistakeConsequence
    Laser-cutting heavily scaled HR without a testInconsistent quality
    Forgetting zinc fumes from galvanizedHealth risk (metal fume fever)
    Ordering “1/4 A36” without a sizeWrong size delivered, more scrap
    Painting over scaleFlaking

    FAQ

    Are A36 and 44W the same?

    Close in use, but different standards (ASTM vs CSA); a lot of plate sold in Canada is dual-certified. If the drawing calls for one, ask for the certificate.

    Why does HRPO cost more?

    Pickling and oiling add a step; in return there’s no scale to remove before laser cutting or painting.
