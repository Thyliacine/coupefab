Calculator: part weight and cost

Weight = density × thickness × part area. Cost = consumed area (part + kerf, then nesting waste) × each supplier’s lowest $/sq ft for that thickness. Plasma: allow 1.5 to 3 mm of kerf depending on nozzle and amperage.

Method

  1. An identified bot (CoupeFabPrixBot) reads suppliers’ public pages daily, one request at a time, honouring robots.txt and its crawl delay. No login, no cart, no bot-protection bypass: a site that refuses is marked “blocked”.
  2. We keep the price as listed (currency, unit, size) and its URL. USD prices are converted at the Bank of Canada daily rate.
  3. For sheet sold by size we keep the largest size offered online (often 48 × 96 in); for bar and tube, the longest length. $/sq ft, $/lb and $/kg are computed.
  4. A row whose $/lb is far from its family median is flagged “to check” and excluded from best prices, but not deleted.
  5. Prices before taxes, freight, cutting and duties. Prices change: always confirm with the supplier.
  6. Licence: the compiled data (table, CSV, JSON, history) is published under CC BY 4.0; reuse freely with credit to CoupeFab and a link to this page. Prices remain those of the cited suppliers.
  7. Frequency: sheet and plate surveyed daily; bar, tube and pipe weekly (each row shows its own date).
  8. Dated history kept in the site’s public repository (one file per day). No affiliate links on this page for now; if that changes they will be labelled and explained in the disclosure.
