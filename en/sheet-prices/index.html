    What do the “Best price”, “Good price”… labels mean?
    • Best price lowest for the spec (≥ 2 suppliers buyable in Quebec)
    • Good price at least 10% below the median
    • Average within ±10% of the median
    • Expensive at least 10% above the median
    • Benchmark benchmark (not buyable in Quebec, or indicative): gap shown, never “best”
    • To check outlier, excluded from comparisons

    The median is taken over each Quebec-buyable supplier’s best unit price for the same spec (same family, sub-class and thickness; $/sq ft for sheet and plate, $/ft or $/lb for bar and tube). With fewer than two suppliers there is no label: we don’t invent a comparison.

    Calculator: part weight and cost

    Weight = density × thickness × area. Cost = consumed area (part + kerf, then nesting waste) × each supplier’s lowest $/sq ft. Plasma: 1.5 to 3 mm of kerf depending on nozzle and amperage. Before taxes, freight and cutting.

    Method

    1. An identified bot (CoupeFabPrixBot) reads suppliers’ public pages, one request at a time, honouring robots.txt and its crawl delay. No login, no cart, no bot-protection bypass: a site that refuses is marked “blocked”.
    2. Frequency: sheet and plate daily; bar, tube and pipe weekly. Each row shows its own date.
    3. We keep the price as listed (currency, unit, size) and its URL. USD → CAD at the Bank of Canada daily rate.
    4. Sheet sold by size: we keep the largest size offered online (often 48 × 96 in); bar and tube: the longest length. $/sq ft, $/lb and $/kg are computed.
    5. Price labels: gap between the unit price and the median of suppliers for the same spec (±10%). Benchmarks (outside Quebec, indicative) and out-of-stock rows can’t be “best price”. An outlier is flagged “to check”, never deleted.
    6. “Buy” and “View at” buttons: direct link to the product page at the supplier (or its row on the category page when the supplier has no per-product page). Plain links, no affiliate parameters; see the disclosure.
    7. Licence: the compiled data (table, CSV, JSON, history) is published under CC BY 4.0; reuse freely with credit to CoupeFab. Prices remain those of the cited suppliers.
    8. Dated history in the site’s public repository (monthly change log). Are you a supplier? Add your prices: once reviewed, they join the price tool with a “Verified prices” badge.
