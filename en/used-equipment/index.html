    Evaluating a used plasma table in Quebec

    Draft · surveyed November 26, 2026 (America/Toronto) · to be reviewed by Michaël

    This page pairs a buyer’s checklist with a first digest of public listings currently online. CoupeFab is not a broker. Prices move, machines sell, and two sites may describe the same table. Always confirm with the seller.

    How the digest is built

    We only link public dealer, auction, or classified pages (for example Machinerie ALX, Industriel Go / Machinio, Tardif Industrie, Ritchie Bros. / IronPlanet). No bulk scraping, no login walls. If no price is posted, we write “ask for price.” The survey date and time zone are listed above.

    Checklist (buying in Quebec)

    • Shop envelope. Useful size versus doors, crane, and height; real voltage (208 / 460 / 600 V, single or three phase); compressed air and dryer; dust collector or water table already in place.
    • Plasma source and torch. Brand and model, amperage, consumable family, hours if they exist, working THC. An advertised “severance capacity” is not production quality — ask to see your typical thickness cut.
    • Mechanics. Backlash in racks or screws, gantry square, rail condition, cables and carriers, rust on the slats, water leaks if it is a water table.
    • Control and licences. Controller (Edge, Vision T5, and so on), backups, CAM software and licences (SheetCam, FastCAM, or other): what is included, what you would have to buy again.
    • History. Retrofits (when, by whom, which parts), parts availability in Canada, original documentation.
    • A test cut. Watch it cut. Measure a coupon (kerf, dross, square). Listen for limit switches and arc faults.
    • Logistics and contract. As-is terms, dismantling, transport, taxes (GST/QST), availability date. An “ex-works” price is not a delivered-in-your-yard price.

    This is a shop-floor canvas, not an inspection report. Michaël should add the points he always checks.
